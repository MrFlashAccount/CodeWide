Three minimal sign systems for an Android-first agentic IDE that turns a remote workstation into a continuous phone, Fold and tablet workspace.

01

Foldline

Recommended

Signature move: one uninterrupted line widens into a Fold-shaped W.

The sign starts as the initial, but earns it: the two outer uprights are phone edges, the inner valley is the hinge, and the open top says the workspace is not contained by one device. It is name-specific, product-specific, and still reads as a clean abstract mark at notification size.

Wordmark treatment

Keep the product spelling CodeWide. W is optically 12% wider; the hidden ide is one weight heavier and slightly tighter. It reveals “IDE” on a second read without turning the name into a typographic riddle.

Light / dark

Ink on warm neutral light; paper-white on OLED black. Cobalt is an icon/launch accent, not an interface primary and never a gradient. In-product brand presence stays monochrome so semantic green/amber/red keep their jobs.

Android adaptive behavior

Foreground fits a 58 × 58 unit box inside Android’s 66 × 66 safe zone. Flat full-bleed background; no baked mask or shadow. The open top and center valley survive circle, squircle, rounded square and themed monochrome.

02

Relay

Strong alternative

Signature move: a narrow device and a wider host workspace share one bridge.

This is the clearest product diagram compressed into a glyph: phone on the left, remote workstation on the right, capability-scoped connection in the middle. The asymmetric widths give it a “CodeWide” expansion rather than a generic sync loop.

Primary light lockup
CodeWide
Android launcher

Wordmark treatment

The canonical spelling stays continuous: CodeWide. “Code” is optically compressed by 8%; “Wide” expands by 6%, repeating the phone-to-workspace transition without inserting punctuation into the name. Hidden ide keeps a quiet weight shift.

Light / dark

Black/white mark first. The solid link green is allowed on launch/marketing surfaces only; avoid it for connected/success state inside the product, where it would collide with existing semantics.

Android adaptive behavior

Both device masses and the bridge stay inside the safe zone. The foreground can move subtly against the background under launcher parallax, but the bridge cannot carry essential state or animation.

03

Wideframe

Editorial alternative

Signature move: four open corners hold one broad working horizon.

The mark treats CodeWide as a viewport, not a destination: the frame is open, the center line is the useful workspace, and nothing resembles a globe, terminal prompt or robot. It has the calmest editorial tone and works well for documentation and open-source presence.

Primary light lockup
CodeWide

Wordmark treatment

Conventional CodeWide silhouette with a short underline only under ide. This makes the hidden IDE discoverable in README/launch contexts while the plain small wordmark drops the underline.

Light / dark

Monochrome is the default. Coral can create a clear open-source/README accent, but should not enter the product’s warning/error vocabulary until semantic conflicts are tested.

Android adaptive behavior

The four corners remain fully inside the safe zone. Do not let OEM mask shape become a fifth “frame”; use enough background contrast and keep the open gaps larger than two physical pixels at target density.

Recommendation

Choose Foldline.

It is the only direction that binds the name, Fold hardware and expanding workspace in one contour without needing the explanation to stay recognizable.

Why it wins

  • Fastest recognition: clear W silhouette at 16 px.
  • Most ownable story: phone edges + Fold hinge + wide workspace.
  • One asset family: launcher, themed icon, notification, favicon and lockup share the same contour.
  • Fits the product: neutral, serious, dense and tool-like rather than mascot-like.

Risks before adoption

  • W monograms are crowded territory; run trademark/similarity search before finalization.
  • The inner valley can resemble a heart at large soft radii; tune joins during final optical drawing.
  • Highlighting “ide” can become gimmicky. Keep the weight delta subtle and drop it below 90 px lockup width.
  • Cobalt is provisional. The repo lacks design law, so palette adoption belongs in a design-contract pass.

Reference scout

  1. Existing CodeWide UI: adopt OLED-neutral contrast, rounded geometry and dense utility tone; reject the current placeholder Android robot/grid and terminal-prompt splash mark.
  2. Android adaptive icon guidance: adopt separate vector foreground/background, a 66 × 66 safe zone and a true monochrome layer; reject baked masks and shadows.
  3. Linear: adopt the discipline of one high-contrast geometric silhouette; reject its diagonal-stripe/metallic language to avoid visual borrowing.
  4. Raycast: adopt app-icon impact through flat, compact geometry; reject glow, bevel and radiating “AI energy”.
  5. Nothing: adopt confidence in typographic rhythm and reduction; reject dot-matrix lettering because it loses sustained readability and does not match the product UI.
